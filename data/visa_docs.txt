If your Visa card was charged twice for the same transaction, the duplicate charge is usually reversed automatically within 3-5 business days.
If a duplicate charge does not reverse on its own, contact your bank or card issuer to file a dispute for the duplicate transaction.
For unauthorized or fraudulent Visa transactions, call the number on the back of your card immediately to report fraud and block the card.
If your Visa card was used without your permission, your bank can initiate a chargeback to recover the funds.
A chargeback request for an unauthorized Visa transaction must typically be filed within 60-120 days of the statement date.
If money was deducted from your account but the payment failed, the hold is usually released within 3-7 business days automatically.
To dispute a Visa transaction, log in to your bank's online portal and navigate to Dispute a Transaction or contact customer support.
If your Visa card was declined, check that the card details are correct, the billing address matches, and the card has not expired.
Visa cards can be temporarily blocked if suspicious activity is detected — contact your bank to unblock it after verifying your identity.
If your Visa card was stolen, report it to your bank immediately to cancel the card and request a replacement.
A replacement Visa card typically arrives within 5-10 business days after reporting it lost or stolen.
If you were overcharged by a merchant, first attempt to resolve it with the merchant directly before filing a dispute with your bank.
Refunds on Visa transactions can take 5-10 business days to appear on your statement after the merchant processes the return.
If a merchant has closed and you are owed a refund, you can file a dispute with your bank for the amount charged.
International Visa transactions may incur foreign transaction fees — check your card agreement for the applicable rate.
If you see a small unfamiliar charge on your Visa statement, it may be a verification charge from a subscription service — check your active subscriptions.
To enable or disable international transactions on your Visa card, contact your bank or update settings in your mobile banking app.
If your Visa card was compromised in a data breach, your bank will typically send a replacement card proactively.
Visa Zero Liability Policy protects you from unauthorized transactions — you are not responsible for fraudulent charges if reported promptly.
If you cancelled a subscription but were still charged, contact the merchant first, then file a dispute with your bank if unresolved within 7 days.
