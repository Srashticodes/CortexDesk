If your iPhone is frozen or unresponsive, perform a force restart by pressing Volume Up, then Volume Down, then holding the Side button until the Apple logo appears.
If your iPhone won't turn on, connect it to a charger for at least 30 minutes before attempting a restart.
If your Apple device was stolen, immediately put it in Lost Mode via Find My at findmy.apple.com to lock it and display a contact number.
To report a stolen Apple device, use Find My to mark it as lost — this also disables Apple Pay on the device.
If your Apple ID was hacked or compromised, go to appleid.apple.com immediately to change your password and enable two-factor authentication.
If you see unauthorized charges on your Apple account, go to reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund for any purchases you did not make.
Apple refunds for unauthorized App Store or iTunes purchases are typically processed within 5-7 business days.
To cancel an Apple subscription, go to Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions and tap the subscription to cancel it.
If you were charged after cancelling an Apple subscription, contact Apple Support with your cancellation date and request a refund.
iCloud storage upgrades can be managed under Settings → Your Name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Change Storage Plan.
If your iCloud is not syncing, check your internet connection and ensure iCloud Drive is enabled under Settings → Your Name → iCloud.
MacBook battery replacement is covered under AppleCare+ — schedule an appointment at an Apple Store Genius Bar or mail it in for repair.
If your MacBook is not charging, try a different cable, reset the SMC, and check for debris in the charging port.
Apple devices under warranty or AppleCare+ are eligible for free hardware repairs for manufacturing defects.
To check your AppleCare+ coverage status, go to mysupport.apple.com or Settings → General → About → Limited Warranty.
If your iPhone screen is cracked, schedule a screen repair at an Apple Store or Apple Authorized Service Provider.
A bricked iPhone that does not respond can often be restored by connecting it to a Mac or PC and using Finder or iTunes to restore it.
If your App Store purchase is not downloading, sign out of your Apple ID and sign back in, then retry the download.
To transfer data to a new iPhone, use iCloud backup or QuickStart — go to Settings → General → Transfer or Reset iPhone.
If you forgot your Apple ID password, go to appleid.apple.com and click Forgot Apple ID or Password to reset it.
If two-factor authentication is locking you out, use account recovery by going to appleid.apple.com and starting the recovery process.
To contact Apple Support, visit support.apple.com or use the Apple Support app to chat, call, or schedule a Genius Bar appointment.
