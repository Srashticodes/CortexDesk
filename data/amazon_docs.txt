If you were charged twice for Amazon Prime, contact Amazon customer support to request a refund for the duplicate charge.
Duplicate or double charges on Amazon are usually reversed within 3-5 business days after being reported to support.
To dispute a charge on Amazon, go to Your Account → Payment Settings → View Transactions → Contact Us.
To track an Amazon order, go to Your Orders and click Track Package next to the item.
If your Amazon package is marked as delivered but you have not received it, wait 48 hours then report it under Your Orders → Problem with Order → Package didn't arrive.
To return an Amazon item, go to Your Orders → Return or Replace Items and follow the steps — most returns are accepted within 30 days.
Amazon refunds are typically processed within 3-5 business days after the return is received.
To cancel Amazon Prime, go to Your Account → Prime Membership → End Membership and follow the prompts.
If you were charged for Amazon Prime after cancelling, contact support with your cancellation confirmation and request a refund.
If you received the wrong item from Amazon, go to Your Orders → Problem with Order → Wrong item was sent to request a replacement or refund.
If your Amazon order is late, check the tracking link in Your Orders for updates or contact the carrier directly.
To report a counterfeit or fake product on Amazon, go to the product listing → Report an issue → This product is counterfeit.
If you were scammed by a third-party seller on Amazon, report the seller and open an A-to-Z Guarantee claim for a refund.
Amazon's A-to-Z Guarantee covers purchases from third-party sellers if the item does not arrive or is significantly not as described.
To update your payment method on Amazon, go to Your Account → Payment Settings → Manage Payment Methods.
If your Amazon account was hacked or compromised, change your password immediately and contact Amazon security support.
To remove an unauthorized device from your Amazon account, go to Account → Content and Devices → Devices and deregister it.
Amazon Prime Video issues such as playback errors can be resolved by clearing app cache or reinstalling the app.
If an Amazon digital purchase such as a Kindle book or app is not downloading, try deregistering and re-registering your device.
To contact Amazon customer support, go to Help → Contact Us and choose chat, email, or callback options.
If your Amazon gift card balance is missing, contact support with the gift card code and proof of purchase.
Amazon Subscribe and Save charges occur automatically — to cancel, go to Your Account → Subscribe and Save → Cancel subscription.
